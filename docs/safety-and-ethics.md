# Safety and Ethics

## Hard lines

- Do not pirate games.
- Do not bypass DRM.
- Do not bypass anti-cheat.
- Do not write multiplayer cheats.
- Do not modify online competitive clients.
- Do not redistribute game files, decompiled source, or extracted proprietary assets.

## Safe default

Mod games you own, in single-player/offline contexts, using official or community-accepted mod loaders.

## Before touching files

- Back up saves.
- Know the install path.
- Know the rollback path.
- Change one thing at a time.
- Keep a mod journal.
